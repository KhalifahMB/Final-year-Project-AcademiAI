# Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University (ATBU), Bauchi — Faculties, Departments & Programmes

**Compiled:** 2026-09-01 · **Sources:** official ATBU website (2023 snapshot via Wayback Machine), Wikipedia, MySchoolGist. All data cross-checked across at least two sources where possible.

## Overview

- **Full name:** Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University
- **Established:** 1980 (as Federal University of Technology, Bauchi; autonomous as ATBU since 1988)
- **Type:** Federal university of technology, Bauchi / North-East Nigeria
- **Accreditation:** National Universities Commission (NUC); member, Association of African Universities
- **Motto:** *Doctrina Mater Artium* — "Education is the mother of the practical arts"
- **Structure (official site):** 7 Faculties offering courses in **43 subject areas**, with degrees in ~399 areas of specialization at diploma, undergraduate, PGD, Master's and Ph.D. levels
- **Students:** ~10,000 undergraduates + ~200 postgraduates (Wikipedia)

## Campuses

- **Gubi (Main) Campus** — Faculty of Agriculture and Agricultural Technology (FAAT), Faculty of Technology Education (FOT), Faculty of Management Technology (FMT), College of Medical Sciences
- **Yelwa Campus** — Faculty of Engineering and Engineering Technology (FEET), Faculty of Environmental Technology (FET), Faculty of Science
